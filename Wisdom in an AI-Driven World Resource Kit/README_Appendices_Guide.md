# Divine Design for the Digital Age
## Companion Resources — Appendix Deck Guide

These seventeen decks turn the book's appendices into ready-to-use PowerPoint resources.
They share the chapter series' warm look but lead with **sage green** to signal that they're
supplementary companion material. Use, adapt, and share them freely in a church or ministry setting.

### How the decks are formatted
Each deck opens with a title slide and a roadmap, then presents its content in the format that
fits it best — teaching-point cards for the essay appendices, question-and-answer slides for the
FAQ, day-by-day cards for the 30-day plan, scored statement rows for the assessment, thematic
commitment cards for the covenant, full-screen prayer slides for the prayer collection, and so on.
Each closes with the series refrain.

### Notes for presenters
- **Appendix E** is the natural hub for running the whole book as a small-group study; its session
  slides pair one-to-one with the fourteen chapter decks.
- **Appendices C, L, and N** are interactive — designed to be worked through, projected during an
  exercise, or printed as handouts.
- **Appendix I** is a reference lookup rather than a talk; keep it on hand while teaching any chapter.
- Fonts are Bookman Old Style (headings) and Calibri (body), both standard with Office.

*Technology will keep changing. The wisdom to navigate it faithfully will not.*
